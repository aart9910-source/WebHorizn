05 Before we begin

Good questions. Straight answers.

Clear expectations make projects easier for everyone. Here is what to know before we start.

Still unsure? Ask on WhatsApp ↗

What is included in each website plan?+

Basic includes 4 pages and 2 rounds of design changes. Standard includes 6 pages and 3 rounds, plus business email for the first year, Google Business Profile setup, logo design and social media links. Both plans include domain and hosting for the first year, SSL, WhatsApp integration, a contact/enquiry form, Google Maps, basic SEO, 30 days of post-launch support, and website ownership after full payment and handover.

What do I need to provide?+

Share your business name and contact details, your logo if you have one, photos, a list of your services, your address for Google Maps, and any text you want on the pages. If you do not have everything ready, tell us and we will guide you.

How long does a website take?+

The current target is 3 days after we receive the required content, assets, payment and approvals. It is a target, not a guarantee; missing material or delayed approvals can move the date.

What happens after the first year?+

Domain and hosting are included for the first year. Standard also includes business email for that year. Provider renewal charges may apply afterward, so ask us about renewal costs before starting.

Will I own my website?+

Yes. Ownership of the agreed website transfers after full payment and handover.

What are design revision rounds?+

A revision round is one consolidated set of design changes, such as text, colours, images or layout. Basic includes 2 rounds and Standard includes 3. New pages or changes to the agreed project scope are quoted separately.

What does 30-day support include?+

It covers help with issues related to the website we built for 30 days after launch. New pages, features or changes beyond the agreed scope are discussed and quoted separately.

How do payments work?+

Payment terms are 50% in advance to begin the project and the remaining 50% before final handover.

How does the enquiry form work?+

It opens WhatsApp with your details prepared as a draft. Your enquiry reaches us only if you press Send in WhatsApp. This website does not save submissions.

Can you build a website for my kind of business?+

We can discuss websites for shops, clinics, coaching institutes, real estate, salons, consultants, freelancers and other service businesses. Tell us about your requirements and we will confirm the fit.

Does basic SEO guarantee Google rankings?+

No. Basic SEO covers setup that helps search engines understand the site. Rankings, enquiries and sales depend on many factors, so we do not guarantee them.
